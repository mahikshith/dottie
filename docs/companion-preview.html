Rendered straight from src/components/ui/creature/geometry.ts — the same numbers the app draws. On the phone these also bob, sway, squash and blink; this page is the pose.

What changed. Sparkles were a full ring of up to twelve dots around the whole body — they are now a fan of five over the crown. Eyes were wide-set black domes; they are closer in with a real catchlight. Head and body were the same width and concentric — there is a neck and a narrower pear body now, plus two planted feet. The owl's two hard dark flanking ellipses are low-contrast crescents that hug the outline. The deer's antler nubs are gone entirely — those were antennae. Fox, cat and bunny gained tails so the shape is no longer mirror-symmetric.
