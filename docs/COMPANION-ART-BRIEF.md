# Dottie — companion art brief (for Gemini)

Six brand-new characters, generated as images, rigged and animated by me afterwards.
Everything below is written to be **copy-pasted into Gemini**, in order.

> **How to use this file:** Section 1 is the style bible — paste it at the top of
> every prompt. Section 2 is the cast. Section 3 is the four sheets to generate per
> character. Section 4 is what to do when Gemini drifts. Section 5 is what to send me.

---

## 0. The one rule that matters

Generate **one character at a time, and approve its hero image before anything else.**
Then attach that approved image to every following prompt and say *"keep this exact
character."* Character consistency is the whole game — six great creatures that don't
look like they come from the same world is worse than six average ones that do.

---

## 1. STYLE BIBLE — paste this at the top of every prompt

```
STYLE BIBLE — "Aurora-born" creature cast for a mobile app.

Art direction: chunky collectible-vinyl creatures, drawn as FLAT VECTOR
illustration — crisp clean edges, solid fills, at most a two-stop gradient per
shape, no texture, no painterly shading, no noise, no gloss beyond the eyes.
Think modern mobile-game mascot: bold simple forms, immediately readable,
designed to be recognised as a solid black silhouette at 28 pixels.

Construction:
- Chibi proportions. Head is 45–50% of total height and slightly WIDER than the
  body. Visible neck. Body is a pear/bean, never the same width as the head.
- Stubby limbs with mitten hands — no fingers, no claws, no elbows.
- Two short legs with simple rounded feet, always visible, never hidden.
- Eyes are large, glossy, dark, set close (about 0.35 of head width apart), each
  with TWO catchlights — one big, one small. This is the single biggest source
  of appeal; do not shrink the eyes.
- Small simple mouth. No visible teeth except in the biggest celebration pose.
- No outlines. Depth comes from a darker tone of the base colour underneath, and
  a soft cool RIM LIGHT down one side, as if lit by an aurora behind the
  character.
- Every character carries ONE asymmetric detail (a bent ear, an off-centre tuft,
  a leaf on one side). Perfect bilateral symmetry is banned — it kills life.
- Glowing bioluminescent freckles or markings in the character's signature
  colour, few and deliberate — this is what ties the cast together.

Palette: the app has a near-black night ground (#0C0A16). Every character must
pop against it, so bodies use mid-to-light values and saturated signature
colours. Accents available: mint #54E6C8, violet #9B7BFF, gold #FFC24D,
rose #FF6FA5.

Hard bans: no outlines, no drop shadow or cast shadow on a ground plane, no
background scenery, no text, labels, captions, watermarks or signatures, no
photo-real fur, no realistic anatomy, no thin appendages (nothing narrower than
6% of body width — it disappears at small sizes), no resemblance to any existing
franchise mascot or Pokémon; these are original characters.

Output: PNG with a fully TRANSPARENT background. Character centred with even
margins, facing the viewer. Square canvas, 2048×2048.
```

## 2. THE CAST — six characters, six hooks

Each has a **shape hook** (so the silhouette is unmistakable) and a **mood hook**
(a body part that changes with feeling, which gives me a whole extra animation
channel beyond the face — a huge deal for a mood-driven app).

| # | Name | What it is | Shape hook | Mood hook | Signature colour |
|---|------|-----------|------------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | **Nimbus** | cloud cub | fluffy scalloped cloud top, tiny legs | drizzles tiny drops when sad, sun-glow when happy | soft lilac-white / violet |
| 2 | **Ember** | flame kit | one big curling flame tail, taller than its head | flame flickers low when tired, flares when excited | orange → coral |
| 3 | **Nori** | tide pup | translucent frills either side of the head, jelly body | frills ripple; body clouds when worried | aqua → deep blue |
| 4 | **Vesper** | moon moth | round wings with crescent-moon markings | crescents dim and brighten; wings fold when thinking | gold on deep indigo |
| 5 | **Fern** | sprout fawn | a single big leaf growing from the head, curled fern tail | leaf droops when low, unfurls when proud | fresh green / mint |
| 6 | **Quartz** | crystal cat | faceted crystal plates along the spine, blunt crystal ears | crystals go dull grey when flat, glow when pleased | rose-quartz pink |

**Personalities** (these drive the poses — mention them in the pose sheet prompt):

1. **Nimbus** — dreamy and slow. Half-asleep even when happy. Bounces gently.
2. **Ember** — eager and impulsive. First to jump, overshoots everything.
3. **Nori** — calm and curious. Watches, tilts its head, moves in float-like arcs.
4. **Vesper** — precise and quietly wise. Small, deliberate movements. Rarely jumps.
5. **Fern** — shy and earnest. Tries hard, celebrates with both hands over its face.
6. **Quartz** — deadpan. Unimpressed at rest, and its celebrations are tiny and dry.

## 3. THE FOUR SHEETS — generate these per character

### Sheet A — hero (do this first, approve it before anything else)

```
[STYLE BIBLE]

Character: NIMBUS, a small cloud cub.

Body: a plump rounded body whose upper half is a soft scalloped cloud shape —
three or four overlapping bumps forming the head and shoulders as one silhouette.
Two very short rounded legs peeking out below. Two stubby mitten arms.
Colour: soft lilac-white body (#EFE9FF) with a violet underside (#9B7BFF) and a
pale mint belly patch. Three tiny glowing violet freckles across the cheeks,
placed asymmetrically.
Face: large glossy dark eyes with two catchlights each, a tiny closed smile,
heavy sleepy upper lids.
Asymmetric detail: one cloud bump on the left sits higher than the rest, with a
single small puff floating just off it.
Expression: relaxed, dreamy, eyes half-lidded.
Pose: standing, front-facing, arms relaxed at its sides, weight on one foot.

Full body, head to feet, nothing cropped. Transparent background. 2048×2048.
```

Repeat for each character, swapping in its row from the table above. Keep every
other word identical — that is what makes them a cast.

### Sheet B — expressions (attach the approved hero)

```
[STYLE BIBLE]

Using the attached image as the EXACT character reference — identical
proportions, colours, markings and features — draw a 4×3 grid of TWELVE
head-and-shoulders portraits of this same character.

Expressions, in reading order:
1 neutral calm   2 warm smile   3 delighted, eyes closed, big open smile
4 proud, chin up  5 curious, head tilted  6 thinking, looking up and away
7 confused, one brow up  8 surprised, eyes wide, small round mouth
9 caring, soft, eyes gently closed  10 worried, brows together
11 sad, eyes down, tiny frown  12 sleepy, eyes almost shut, small yawn

Every portrait is the same size, same head position and same camera distance in
its cell. Wide even spacing between cells. No grid lines, no borders, no numbers,
no captions, no text of any kind. Transparent background.
```

### Sheet C — poses (attach the approved hero)

```
[STYLE BIBLE]

Using the attached image as the EXACT character reference, draw a 4×2 grid of
EIGHT full-body poses of this same character. This character is dreamy and slow —
its movements are gentle and floaty, never sharp.

Poses, in reading order:
1 standing idle, relaxed
2 crouched, about to jump (anticipation — knees bent, arms back)
3 mid-air at the top of a small hop, arms up, feet tucked
4 landing, knees bent, body squashed slightly wide
5 big celebration, both arms overhead, mouth open, leaning back
6 waving with one arm, other hand near its chest
7 thinking — one hand touching its cheek, weight on one hip
8 sad slump — shoulders down, arms hanging, head lowered

All eight at exactly the same scale, standing on the same invisible baseline,
front-facing. Wide even spacing. No grid lines, no borders, no text, no shadows
on the ground. Transparent background.
```

### Sheet D — parts (the one I actually rig — attach the approved hero)

```
[STYLE BIBLE]

Using the attached image as the EXACT character reference, draw this character
DISASSEMBLED into separate parts, laid out flat and spread apart on one canvas
like pieces of a paper puppet. Nothing overlapping, generous space around every
piece, every piece at the same scale as the reference.

Parts required, each drawn complete and whole (draw the hidden portions too, as
if the part were lifted away from the character):
- head (without ears or headpiece)
- left ear / headpiece, right ear / headpiece — separate pieces
- left eye, right eye — separate
- left brow, right brow — separate
- four mouth shapes: small smile, open happy smile, small frown, small "o"
- torso / body
- belly patch
- left arm with hand attached, right arm with hand attached — separate
- left leg with foot, right leg with foot — separate
- tail or signature feature (flame, frills, wings, leaf, crystals) as its own piece
- any floating extras (puffs, sparkles) as separate pieces

No labels, no text, no arrows, no numbers, no outlines around the layout.
Transparent background. 2048×2048.
```

## 4. WHEN GEMINI DRIFTS — repair phrases that work

| What went wrong | Say this |
|---|---|
| Character changed between sheets | "This is a different character. Match the attached reference exactly: same head-to-body ratio, same colours by hex, same markings in the same places. Change only the pose." |
| Added an outline | "Remove all outlines and strokes. Flat shapes with no border lines." |
| Added a ground shadow | "Remove the shadow and the ground plane completely. Nothing under the feet. Fully transparent background." |
| Background isn't transparent | "Output PNG with a real alpha channel and no background at all. If transparency is impossible, use a flat solid #101018 background with no gradient or texture." |
| Put text/labels on a sheet | "Remove every letter, number, caption and label from the image." |
| Eyes went small / dead | "Make the eyes much larger and glossier, about 0.28 of head width each, with two white catchlights per eye — one large upper-left, one small lower-right." |
| Fingers appeared | "Hands are simple mittens with no fingers or thumbs." |
| Went painterly / textured | "Flat vector illustration. Solid fills only, crisp clean edges, no brush texture, no noise, no soft airbrush shading." |
| Cast doesn't look related | "Match the attached three images as one cast: same eye style, same mitten hands, same rim-light direction, same glowing freckle treatment." |

## 5. WHAT TO SEND ME

Per character, four PNGs, named exactly:

```
nimbus_hero.png      nimbus_expressions.png   nimbus_poses.png   nimbus_parts.png
ember_hero.png       ember_expressions.png    ...
nori_… vesper_… fern_… quartz_…
```

- PNG, transparent background, 2048px on the long edge.
- **`_parts.png` is the important one.** With it I can rotate limbs from their
  joints, swap eyes and mouths per emotion, tint per mood, and animate every
  state we have from one drawing — anticipation, squash and stretch, overshoot,
  ears and tails lagging a beat behind the body, eye darts, irregular blinks.
- If you can only get one sheet per character, send `_parts.png`.
- Don't bother with video or animation from Gemini — the motion is mine to build,
  and a video can't be rigged.

**Optional but very useful:** run `_hero.png` and `_parts.png` through a free
vectoriser (Adobe Express, vectorizer.ai, or Inkscape's Trace Bitmap) and send the
SVGs too. Flat vector art traces cleanly, and vectors let me recolour a character
per mood, keep the APK small, and stay sharp at any size.

## 6. Review loop

Send whatever you get and I'll put every character into a browser page — all six,
every expression, every state, animating — that you open in a second. No 25-minute
APK round to look at art. We iterate there until you're happy, and only then does
it go near a build.
