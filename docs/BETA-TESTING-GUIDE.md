# 🌱 Dottie Beta — Tester's Guide

Hi! You're one of the first 100 people in the world to try **Dottie**, a warm, joyful cycle-tracking companion. Thank you for being here — your honest feedback is what shapes the version that millions of women will eventually use. 💛

This guide covers everything you need to know to test Dottie well.

---

## 💌 Your most important job: feedback

You'll see a small 💌 floating button in the bottom-right corner of every screen in the app. Tap it any time something:

- Makes you smile 😊
- Confuses you 🤔
- Feels broken 🐛
- Could be better 💡
- Reminds you of something amazing or something terrible

When you tap it:
1. A friendly form slides up.
2. Pick how you're feeling about the app (😞 → 🥰).
3. Type whatever's on your mind.
4. Optionally include your email if you'd like a reply.
5. Tap **Send to Dottie team** — your email app opens with everything pre-filled.
6. Hit Send in your email app. We get it. 💛

**Nothing is sent anywhere until you tap Send in your own email.** Your feedback is private until you choose to share it.

## 🔧 Common questions

**Q: I want to start over completely.**  
A: Profile → Settings → Delete my data. This wipes your local DB + MMKV + all logs.

**Q: I'm scared someone will see my cycle data on my phone.**  
A: Set up Ghost Mode (Profile → Ghost Mode). Pick a PIN. Now Dottie won't open without it. Turn on the **disguise** option and it'll even pose as a plant-care notes app.

**Q: My period prediction looks wrong.**  
A: The prediction engine gets MUCH more accurate after 2-3 logged cycles. If you're new, predictions are mostly placeholders. If you're a few cycles in and still off, please send us a feedback note describing the gap — that's gold for our algorithm work.

**Q: I can't see my friend's cycle in our Sisterhood circle.**  
A: That's by design. Each member chooses their own privacy level (Full / Summary / Mood Only / Connected). Ask them to check **Sisterhood → their name → Privacy**.

**Q: Will my data be deleted when the beta ends?**  
A: No. Your local data persists across beta updates. If we ever ship a breaking schema change, we'll guide you through migration. We will NEVER silently wipe your data.
